Fishing gear guide

Freshwater or saltwater?

Start with where you fish. Water type changes the right rod, reel, line, terminal tackle, tools and storage.

Freshwater setup

Lighter, flexible and species-focused

  • Match rod power and action to the fish and lure weight.
  • Choose mono, fluorocarbon or braid for cover, clarity and technique.
  • Organize hooks, weights, soft plastics and smaller lures by use.
Compare freshwater combos ↗
Saltwater setup

Corrosion-resistant and built for tougher conditions

  • Look for sealed or corrosion-resistant reel components.
  • Match heavier line and leader to structure, species and current.
  • Prioritize rinse-friendly tools, waterproof storage and secure tackle.

Field checklist

Five hunting-gear checks before you buy.

1. Match the weatherLayering, rain protection, mobility and quiet materials should fit the conditions.
2. Match the distancePack capacity, support, hydration and access depend on trip length and terrain.
3. Check the detailsFor optics and electronics, confirm waterproofing, batteries and included accessories.
4. Read the listingCheck exact condition, seller feedback, shipping, returns and what is actually included.
5. Follow local rulesHunting, equipment and knife laws vary. Confirm the rules where you will use the gear.
